Tech hero

Ayah Bdeir

Ayah Bdeir is a Lebanese engineer and inventor who founded littleBits, a company that makes electronic building blocks. The pieces connect with magnets, letting beginners experiment with circuits without starting with complicated wiring. Her work makes building with technology feel more approachable.

Her connection to equity is especially clear in Snap the Gap. In 2019, littleBits, Disney, and UC Davis announced a program designed to support girls in STEM. Its first-year pilot aimed to reach 15,000 girls in California with invention kits, a learning community, and mentorship.

This work challenges the idea that engineering is mainly for boys or people who already know how to code. Giving girls tools and support early can help them see themselves as inventors. It also addresses a practical part of inclusion: encouragement matters, but people need opportunities to experiment too.

Bdeir is an example of an Arab woman whose work connects engineering with wider access to technology in the United States. Her approach shows how the design of a learning tool can influence who feels welcome using it.
