Welcome to my website

Welcome to my website. My name is Naser Edreis, and I am a senior studying computer science.

This site looks at technology and the people it affects. It includes an introduction, resources about implicit bias and intersectionality, and a profile of someone making technology more welcoming.

What is on this site?
